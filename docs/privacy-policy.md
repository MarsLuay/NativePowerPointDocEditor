# Privacy Policy

**Native PowerPoint Doc Editor** is an Obsidian community plugin. This policy describes what information the plugin handles, why, and your choices. It applies to the plugin software distributed through Obsidian's community catalog or manual installation in your vault.

Last updated: June 22, 2026.

## What information we collect

The plugin is **local-first**. It does not operate a server account for you and does not collect profile details or usage analytics.

Information the plugin may store or process on your device includes:

- **Vault documents** you open, edit, or save (DOCX, PPTX, and related formats).
- **Plugin settings** you configure in Obsidian (for example editor toggles, optional DOCX search indexing, and diagnostics preferences).
- **Optional local caches**, such as the DOCX search index file when you enable that feature.
- **Recovery copies** written locally if a PowerPoint save fails or autosave is disabled.
- **Files you choose** through system pickers when importing fonts or inserting images; only the content needed for your document is used.
- **Debug diagnostics** you explicitly copy to the clipboard when developer logging is enabled.

We do not ask you to create an account to use this plugin.

## Why we collect it and how we use your information

We use the information above only to:

- Open, render, edit, search, save, and export your documents inside Obsidian.
- Remember your plugin settings between sessions.
- Provide optional features you turn on (such as vault-wide DOCX search).
- Help you recover from failed saves when recovery behavior applies.
- Show diagnostics you request for troubleshooting.

We do not use your vault contents for advertising or profiling.

## Analytics, advertising, cookies, and third-party SDKs

- **No telemetry or analytics.** The plugin does not phone home or embed analytics SDKs.
- **No advertising.** The plugin does not load ad scripts or remote UI content.
- **No cookies.** The plugin does not set browser cookies; Obsidian manages its own application data separately.

## Who we share information with

We do **not** sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes.

Your data stays on your device except when **you** choose an action that leaves the vault, for example:

- Opening a hyperlink inside a document (handled by Obsidian or your operating system).
- Following outbound links shown in settings (for example GitHub issue reporting).
- Exporting a DOCX to PDF when the document references remote image URLs (your browser may fetch those URLs to render the export).

The plugin does not upload vault contents to our servers as part of normal editing, search, save, or export.

## Retention

Data retention is **local-first** and under your control:

- Edited documents remain in your vault until you change or delete them.
- Plugin settings persist until you reset or uninstall the plugin.
- Optional search index data remains until you disable indexing or delete the index file.
- Debug log files are written only when a developer hot-reload marker is present; otherwise logs stay in memory and the developer console.

## Access, deletion, and your choices

Because processing happens on your device:

- **Access** your documents and plugin data through Obsidian and your vault folder.
- **Delete** documents, index files, recovery copies, or plugin data at any time using Obsidian or your file manager.
- **Uninstall** the plugin to remove its settings from Obsidian's plugin storage.
- **Disable** optional features (such as DOCX search indexing) in plugin settings.

You may request a copy of information we hold only if you contact us about support for this open-source project; we do not maintain a separate cloud copy of your vault.

## Whether we sell or share information

**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
